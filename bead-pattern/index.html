Tutorial

Create your first daisy

A simple daisy has been loaded on the grid. Here's how it was made:

1
Pick a color & place beads

Click the colored dot next to Bead in the dock to pick a color, then click cells on the grid.

2
Connect with wires

Switch to the Wire tool, click a bead, then click another to draw a wire between them.

3
Add loops

Use the Loop tool — click a cell to create a ring of beads connected by wires.

4
Change shapes

Click the arrow next to Bead in the dock to pick other shapes: diamond, star, hexagon…

5
Export your pattern

Click the export icons in the dock to download your design as a PNG.
