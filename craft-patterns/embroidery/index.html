Tutorial

Create your first pattern

A simple flower has been loaded on the grid. Here's how it was made:

1
Pick a color & place stitches

Press S or click Stitch. Click the color dot to pick a thread color, then click cells on the grid.

2
Change stitch type

Click the arrow next to Stitch to choose: cross stitch, half cross, French knot, straight…

3
Select & move stitches

Press A to select an area, drag to move it. Press M to move individual stitches.

4
Rotate a motif

Press R then click a stitch to rotate its connected group 90°.

5
Export your pattern

Click the export icons in the dock to download as PNG. Ctrl+Z to undo, E to erase.
