Tutorial

Your first peyote pattern

A sample pattern has been loaded. Here's how the peyote editor works:

1
The staggered grid

Each oval represents one bead. Odd rows are offset by half a bead — this is the peyote/brick stitch structure used in real beadwork.

2
Place beads

Click the color dot to pick a color, then click or drag on the canvas to place beads. Press B to activate the bead tool.

3
Erase beads

Press E or click the eraser in the dock. Click or drag over beads to remove them.

4
Undo & redo

Press Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo. Use the buttons top-right too.

5
Export your pattern

Click the export icon in the dock to download your pattern as a PNG image.
